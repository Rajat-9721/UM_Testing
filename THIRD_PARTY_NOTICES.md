# Third-party notices

## Resumify

The Resume Generator's template system (`astro-site/src/lib/resume/`) is adapted from the
architecture of **Resumify** — https://github.com/Afif718/Resumify — specifically its separation of
resume data from template configuration (`TEMPLATE_CONFIGS`), reorderable/hideable sections and
categorised skills. The implementation was rewritten for this portal (Astro + TypeScript, ATS-focused
templates, PDF/DOCX export); no Resumify UI components are embedded.
