# Leads — guide for assistants

Every time someone fills a form on the website (brochure download, free demo lecture,
next batch alert), it appears here. No technical knowledge needed.

## Open it

Log in → **Assistant Dashboard** → **Leads** in the left menu.

## What you see

- **Three boxes on top:** new leads nobody has contacted yet, follow-ups due today (or
  overdue), and how many enquiries came in the last 7 days.
- **The table:** newest first.
  - **Contact:** tap the number to call, **WhatsApp** to chat, or the email to write.
  - **Verified** (green) means the person confirmed their email with a code, so the email
    is real. **Not verified** means they used a form without a code.
  - **Program / Form:** which program and which form they used.
  - **Status:** change it right in the table — it saves by itself.
  - **Follow-up:** a red date means it's due today or overdue.
- **Details** opens everything about the lead, plus the follow-up date and notes.

## A simple daily routine

1. Choose **New** in the status filter. Call or WhatsApp each person.
2. Change the status after each call (see the meanings below).
3. If they say "call me later": **Details** → pick a **Follow-up date**, write a short
   note (e.g. "Call after 6pm, asking about weekend batch") → **Save**.
4. Every morning tick **Follow-ups due** to see who to call today.

## What the statuses mean

| Status | Use it when |
|---|---|
| New | Not contacted yet (every new lead starts here) |
| Contacted | You spoke to them or they replied |
| Not reachable | Call not answered, busy or switched off |
| Follow-up | You need to call again on the follow-up date |
| Interested | They want to join and are deciding |
| Demo scheduled | A demo lecture date is fixed |
| Enrolled | They joined a program |
| Not interested | They decided not to join |
| Invalid / junk | Wrong number, fake or test entry |

## Download Excel

**Download Excel** saves the leads you are currently looking at (with the filters you
picked) as a file that opens in Excel or Google Sheets. It is a copy: changes made in the
file do **not** come back to the dashboard — always update status and notes in the
dashboard.

## Please keep this data private

These are people's phone numbers and emails. Don't forward the Excel file to anyone
outside the team, don't post it in groups, and delete downloaded copies you no longer
need.
